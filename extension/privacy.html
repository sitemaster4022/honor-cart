Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

This packaged copy explains HonorCart version 1.0.0. The current public policy is available at honorcart.com/privacy.

Information processed

The extension locally processes the active page address to recognize supported merchants and known affiliate-referral parameters. It stores consent and per-tab attribution state using browser extension storage. Per-tab session state is deleted when the tab closes.

After consent, the extension contacts our service for merchant configuration. When you request coupons, it transmits the merchant hostname and path. Only when you explicitly request affiliate activation does it transmit the merchant hostname and page address with query parameters and fragments removed.

Purposes and sharing

Information is used only to provide coupon discovery, protect detected referrals, operate approved affiliate activation, secure the service, and comply with law. We do not sell personal information or browsing data, and do not use it for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising. We share the minimum required activation information with the applicable affiliate network or merchant only after your explicit action.

Retention and security

HonorCart does not intentionally retain shopper browsing activity or extension telemetry in this release. Cloudflare may process standard request and security metadata as described in the public policy. Production transmission uses HTTPS, and access is limited to operating and securing the service.

Your controls

You may withdraw consent and clear locally stored extension data at any time from the extension's “Privacy & controls” page. You may also uninstall the extension. Contact us using the addresses below to request access to or deletion of information you submitted directly.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Email privacy@honorcart.com for privacy requests or support@honorcart.com for product help.
