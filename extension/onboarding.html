Coupons without silently replacing referrals

HonorCart checks the current shopping address on your device for known affiliate signals. When one is detected, our monetization control is disabled for that session.

Data used to provide the service

  • On your device: the current page address is checked to identify supported merchants and referral parameters. Per-tab state disappears when the tab closes.
  • Sent when you request coupons: the merchant hostname and path are sent to our service to retrieve available coupons.
  • Sent when you explicitly activate: the merchant hostname and page address without its query string or fragment are sent to create an approved affiliate activation.

We do not sell browsing data, use it for personalized advertising, or transmit a general browsing history. Coupon lookup and activation remain unavailable until you consent below.

Affiliate disclosure

When you deliberately select “Activate savings & support us,” we may earn a commission from an approved merchant at no added cost to you. Finding coupons alone does not claim affiliate attribution.
