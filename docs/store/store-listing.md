# Store listing copy

## Product name

HonorCart

## Summary

Find coupons while protecting detected creator and affiliate referrals.

## Single purpose

Help shoppers retrieve available coupon codes for the merchant they are currently visiting, while preventing this extension from monetizing a session when a supported existing affiliate referral is detected.

## Detailed description

HonorCart helps you check for available coupon codes at supported online stores. The initial release supports a controlled HonorCart reviewer environment while merchant approvals are completed; no merchant session is currently monetized.

Our referral-safe design is visible and deliberate:

- Referral checks occur locally on your device.
- If a supported creator or affiliate referral signal is detected, our monetization control is disabled for that shopping session.
- Looking up coupons does not claim affiliate attribution.
- We may earn a commission only after you deliberately select “Activate savings & support us,” and only when that merchant has approved our extension traffic.
- A server-controlled allowlist and kill switch can disable monetization immediately.

On first use, the extension explains the data needed for coupon retrieval and asks for consent. It does not sell browsing data or use it for personalized advertising. Merchant support is intentionally limited while approvals are added.

## Category

Shopping

## Affiliate disclosure for listing

HonorCart is designed to participate in approved affiliate programs. Affiliate monetization is disabled in the initial release. If it is enabled for an approved merchant in a future update, the developer may earn a merchant commission only after no supported existing referral is detected and you deliberately select the activation control. Coupon lookup alone does not activate an affiliate relationship.
